What to know about this

SANGAH is the a traditional meal originating from the east region and mostly from the maka and beti group of cameroon.

it is made of Corn, cassava leaves, and palm nut juice mixed together into a simple one-pot dish.

Informations about this

KOKI is one of the most appreciated meals in cameroon. Its originates from the grassfields and western region of cameroon.

It is made from mashed black-eyed peas or cowpeas mixed with palm oil and chili, wrapped and steamed in banana leaves.
